About EBAL Innotech

Transforming Learning into Real Industry Experience

EBAL Innotech is an industry-integrated experiential learning platform designed to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world engineering applications. We empower Mechanical, Electronics, Mechatronics, and Manufacturing graduates with industry skills that prepares them for core industry roles.
